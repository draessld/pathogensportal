## Processing of personal data

This page describes what we learn about visitors to the portal, why, how long we keep
it, and what you can do about it.

**The portal uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device.** That is why there is
no consent banner — there is nothing to consent to. The duty to inform remains, and
that is what this page is for.

## What is processed

| What | Where it comes from | How long | Personal data? |
|---|---|---|---|
| IP address, time, page address, browser type, referring page | web server access log | **14 days**, then deleted | **yes** |
| Visit counts by organisation and day | aggregated statistics | indefinitely | **no** — the address is resolved to an organisation and discarded, never stored |
| Which pages are read, how people move through the site | web analytics (Umami, run by us) | per the tool's settings | **no** — no cookies, no device identifier |

⚠️ **An IP address is personal data** even when we do not know the name behind it — this
follows from the Court of Justice of the EU judgment in C-582/14 (Breyer). A web server
access log is therefore processing of personal data, independently of any analytics.

## Why we process it

- **Operation and security.** Without the log there is no way to tell that the server is
  broken, or to recognise an attack. The overwhelming majority of traffic to this portal
  today is automated scanning.
- **Demonstrating usefulness.** The portal is publicly funded and we need to be able to
  show who uses it — at the level of **institutions, not individuals**.

The legal basis is the controller's **legitimate interest** (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in secure
operation and in evaluating the usefulness of the service.

## What we deliberately do not do

- ⛔ We do not track individual visitors across visits or across sites.
- ⛔ We do not pass data to third parties. The analytics runs **on our own server**, not
  at an external service — the data does not leave it.
- ⛔ We do not profile and do not make automated decisions.
- ⛔ We do not store IP addresses in the statistics. An address is resolved to an
  organisation (for example "Charles University") and only counts are kept.

## How long

The access log is deleted after **14 days**. Aggregated counts by organisation are kept
indefinitely — they are not personal data, because nothing in them leads back to a
particular person or device.

## Your rights

You have the right of access to your data, to rectification or erasure, to restriction of
processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to lodge a complaint
with the [Czech Data Protection Authority](https://uoou.gov.cz).

⚠️ **A practical note:** we cannot find your records in the access log unless you tell us
the IP address and roughly when you visited — we keep nothing else that could be searched
on. That is a consequence of not collecting more than we need.
